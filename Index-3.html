Pumpkin Soup

This is THE pumpkin soup recipe you will make now and forever! It's thick, creamy, and irresistable, a classic wasy pumpkin soup made with fresh pumpkin that's fast to make, and only 189 calories per serving. Don't forget crusty bread for dunking!

Main Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg / 2.4 lb Pumpkin (Any type)
  • 1 Onion, sliced (White, Brown, Yellow)
  • 2 Garlic Cloves, (Peeled whole).
  • 750 ml Vegetable or Chicken broth/stock, (Low Sodium)
  • 250 ml Water
  • Salt and Pepper

Finishes

  • 125-180 ml Cream, Half and Half or Milk

Instructions

  1. Cut the Pumpkin into 3cm/2.25" slices. Cut the skin off and scrape out seeds. Cut into 4cm /1.5" chunks.
  2. Place the Pumpkin, Onion, Garlic, Broth and Water in a pot - Liquid won't quite cover all the Pumpkin. Bring to a boil, uncovered, then reduce heat and let simmer rapidly until Pumpkin is tender (Check with butter knife) - about 10 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and use a stick blender to blend until smooth.
  4. Season to taste with Salt and Pepper, stir through Cream (Never boil soup after adding Cream, the Cream will split).
  5. Ladle soup into bowls, drizzle a bit of Cream over-top, sprinkle with Pepper, and Parsley if desired. Serve with Crusty Bread!

Pecan Pie

This Easy old-fashioned Pecan Pie recipe is the BEST, with it's rich caramel pecan filling paired with a perfect flakey and buttery crust.

Main Ingredients

  • 1 Homemade Pie Crust, unbaked (My recipe makes 2 crusts, so you can freeze the other crust).
  • 200 g Granulated Sugar.
  • 40 g Light Brown Sugar.
  • 3 g Salt.
  • 240 ml Light Corn Syrup.
  • 3.75 ml Vanilla Extract.
  • 75 g Salted Butter (Softened or Melted).
  • 3 Eggs.
  • 150 g Pecan Halves, (Can chop them or leave them whole).

Instructions

Perfect Pie Crust Recipe
  1. Prepare my perfect Pie Crust recipe and place pie dough in a deep dish pie plate. Refrigerate crust while you prepare the filling.
  2. In a large bowl cream together Sugar, Brown Sugar, Salt, Corn Syrup, and Softened/Melted Butter. Mix in the Eggs and Vanilla.
  3. Reserve a few Pecans and stir the rest into the batter. Pour into unbaked pie shell. Add a few of the reserved pecans on-top of the pie in any emoty spots, to malke it look pretty. Use a small spoon to cover them with batter.
  4. Bake in oven at 425f for 10 minutes, then lower the oven temp to 350f and continue baking for about 50 minutes. Check the Pie half-way through cooking and tent a large piece of greased aluminium foil over the top of the Pie. (Tent it high so it doesn't touch the filling). You will know your Pie is done when you can shake it gently and its not overly jiggly. If it jiggles alot when shaking, continue baking until the center is more set, 5-10 more minutes, or longer if needed. Err on the side of cooking longer than shorter to ensure that the middle has set.
  5. Allow the Pie to cool Completely for several hours, before cutting and serving. Or refrgerate it for 1-3 days, until ready to serve.

Spiced Maple Bourbon Fizz

This Spiced Maple Bourbon Fizz is the perfect Cozy Cocktail. Homemade spices maple syrup, mixed with the warming bourbon, lemon, and orange, then topped off with bubbly champagne for that special fall touch. Perfect for the upcomiong thanks giving and cold nights ahead. Make a large batch to serve to family and friends over the holidays, this is so easy to create!

Main Ingredients

Spiced Maple Syrup

  • 113 g Water
  • 113 g Maple Syrup.
  • 2 Cinnamon Sticks.
  • 1 TBSP Cardamon Pods.
  • 1 Star Anise.
  • 1" Fresh Ginger (Sliced).
  • Zest of 1 Orange.

Maple Fizz

  • 3 TBSP Bourbon.
  • 2 TBSP Lemon Juice.
  • 1-2 Dashes Orange Bitters.
  • 2 TBSP Spiced Maple Syrup.
  • Champagne for Topping

Instructions

  1. To make the Spiced Maple Syrup. In a Medium pot, bring Water, the Maple, Cinnamon, Cardamon, Star Anise, and Ginger to boil over High heat. Boil for 2-3 minutes, then remove from the heat. Stir in the Orange Zest. Let cool, then strain the Syrup into a glass jar. This Syrup makes enoigh for about 6 Drinks.
  2. In a Cocktail shaker, Combine Maple Syrup, the Bourbon, Lemon Juice, and Orange bitters. Add ice and shake. Strain into a glass. Top off with Champagne. Garnish with an Orange Slice and Star Anise, if desired.
